Topic Cards

Biopharmaceutics and Pharmacokinetics, ADME

Absorption

Movement of drug from the site of administration into systemic circulation.

Pharmacokinetics, ADME

Distribution

Reversible transfer of drug between blood and body tissues after absorption or IV entry.

Pharmacokinetics, ADME, Biotransformation

Metabolism

Enzymatic chemical conversion of drugs, mainly in liver, into metabolites.

Pharmacokinetics, ADME

Excretion

Removal of unchanged drug and metabolites from the body, mainly by kidney and bile.

Pharmacokinetics, One-compartment model

Intravenous Bolus Injection

Rapid injection of an IV dose directly into systemic circulation.

Pharmacokinetics, input-rate models

Intravenous Infusion and Extravascular Administration

Drug input by constant IV infusion or by absorption from extravascular routes such as oral or IM.

Advanced pharmacokinetics and compartment modeling

Multi-Compartment Models

Models where drug moves between central and peripheral compartments with different rates.
